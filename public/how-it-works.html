How Gorse Quill works

Gorse Quill keeps one short list and shows it to everyone, the moment it changes. Three steps cover it.

  1. Add an item to the shared list from any device.
  2. Check it off once it is done; the list updates for everyone.
  3. Review the five most recent items on the landing page.

Items are kept in the shared project and shown newest first.

Every visitor sees the same list, so there is nothing to sign in to and nothing to set up before you start.
